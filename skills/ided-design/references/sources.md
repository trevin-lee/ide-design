# Sources

The guidance in this skill is ided's own writing, informed by these works. Read them for depth.

## Design practice and craft

- Massimo Vignelli, *The Vignelli Canon* (free PDF from RIT's Vignelli Center).
- Josef Müller-Brockmann, *Grid Systems in Graphic Design*.
- Robert Bringhurst, *The Elements of Typographic Style*; webtypography.net applies it to screens.
- Matthew Butterick, *Practical Typography* (practicaltypography.com).
- Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger, *Refactoring UI*.
- Josef Albers, *Interaction of Color*. Johannes Itten, *The Art of Color* (contrast of extension).
- Edward Tufte, *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* (data-ink).
- Michael Alley, assertion-evidence slides (Penn State). Nancy Duarte, the glance test and
  *Resonate* (the "what is / what could be" contrast).
- Bob Gill, *Forget All the Rules You Ever Learned About Graphic Design*.
- Michael Bierut, "On (Design) Bullshit" (Design Observer). Mike Monteiro, *Design Is a Job*.
- Michael Johnson, *Branding: In Five and a Half Steps*.
- Paul Rand, "Logos, Flags, and Escutcheons" and the NeXT presentation booklet.
- Chermayeff, Geismar & Haviv, *Identify*, and Sagi Haviv's interviews on what makes a mark last.
- Design Council, the Double Diamond. Liz Lerman, Critical Response Process. Edmund Feldman's
  method of art criticism (describe, analyze, interpret, judge).
- Nielsen Norman Group on Gestalt proximity, common region and the squint test.
- Case studies from Pentagram, Collins, Wolff Olins and Koto, for how identity rationales are
  presented.

## Agent skills and prompting research

Techniques adapted in spirit (no text copied):

- Anthropic, `frontend-design` and `canvas-design` skills (github.com/anthropics/skills,
  Apache-2.0) and "Improving frontend design through Skills": named output clusters, a plan
  reviewed for genericness before building, one bold move, the subtractive final pass.
- Paul Bakaus, Impeccable (github.com/pbakaus/impeccable, Apache-2.0): naming the category's rut
  and its opposite, candidates from the audience's cultural world, color strategy before colors,
  external randomness, the skeleton and memory tests.
- design-director (github.com/j-withmission/design-director) and world-class-designer
  (github.com/elammertsma/world-class-designer), both MIT, after Anshu Chimala's process: a
  screenshot-only critic in a fresh context, KEEP lists and verdicts, pairwise rather than
  absolute judgment, seeded axes.
- Hallmark (github.com/Nutlope/hallmark, MIT): a catalog of named structures chosen before
  styling, honest copy.
- OpenAI `frontend-skill` (github.com/openai/skills, Apache-2.0): a visual thesis before building,
  the first viewport as a poster, "if deleting copy improves it, keep deleting".
- frontend-slides (github.com/zarazhangrui/frontend-slides, MIT): speaker-led versus read-alone
  density decided up front; split rather than shrink.
